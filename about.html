Our story

Built by engineers, for builders

We started as a small team of engineers who got tired of watching good ideas fail due to poor technical execution. Over the years, we've helped startups find product-market fit, helped enterprises modernize legacy systems, and helped everything in between ship software they're proud of.

Our model is simple: small teams, senior talent, real ownership. We don't send junior consultants to learn on your dime.

How we work

Principles we don't compromise on

Directness

We tell you what we think, even when it's not what you want to hear. Honest feedback early is cheaper than silent agreement later.

Ownership

We treat your codebase like our own. That means caring about long-term quality, not just closing the ticket.

Simplicity

The best solution is usually the simplest one that works. We resist over-engineering and chase clarity instead.

Continuity

We build systems and teams that can operate without us — documentation, tests, and knowledge transfer are non-negotiable.
